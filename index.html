Sudhu Tomar Jonno – Ekta Shantir Chithi

Jodi kokhono mon-ta khub bhari lage, matha jure jay ba kichu bhalo na lage—tumi nijer bhetore chup kore sob chepe rekho na. Ekhane ektu bosho, shwash nao, kono tara nei.

Tumi je bhabe nijer moner bhar samle niye cholo, seta sotti khub sundor. Sob kichu ekshathe thik kore felar kono chap nio na—dhire dhire sob thik hoye jabe.

Jokhon-i ektu shanti, ekta warm hug ba ekta choto smile dorkar hobe, mone rekho ami sob somoy tomar pashe achi. Tumi ektu haslei amar puro din-ta onno rokom bhalo hoye jay.
